Welcome to Java Programming: Object-Oriented Foundations. Everything in this course is built on one book and one idea: software is a set of objects providing services to each other. Over sixteen weeks we move from basic object concepts to polymorphism, contracts, and design patterns. Keep the book at hand — every lecture maps onto chapters one through nine. By week 16 you will design and defend your own polymorphic system. Let me show you the rhythm of the course.
