Here is the course info and our weekly rhythm. Every week pairs a one-hour lecture with a one-hour lab, and the pairing matters: every lab reuses the classes built in lecture, so skipping lecture makes lab twice as hard. The textbook is Thinking in Java, fourth edition, chapters one through nine. All slide code is verified on OpenJDK seventeen, and labs build on the previous week's classes, so falling behind compounds quickly. Office hours are posted on the course site. One practical note for Chinese-locale Windows machines: compile with javac -encoding UTF-8 to avoid charset trouble. Here is the whole course on one slide.
