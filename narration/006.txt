Let me start with the maintenance horror story. In a procedural style, data lives over here and functions over there, and in big programs global state ends up everywhere — one change breaks everything. The OOP answer: ask objects for services, not for their data, and design programs that survive change. Look at the two sketches: the tangle on the left, the clean service conversations on the right. If you want optional depth, read section 1.7 of the book on containers and Java and the Internet — it is not examined. The tool that tames this problem is abstraction.
