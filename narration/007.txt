Abstraction is the first move: extract the essential, ignore the irrelevant. Every object becomes a service provider. Here is a sixty-second demo — take a problem statement like course registration and pull out candidate objects: Student, Course, Registrar, each with two services. One warning from the term pack: abstract does not mean vague — it means selective. Also notice the -tion and -ment nominalizations: implement becomes implementation, and they recur in error messages all semester. So what exactly is an object?
