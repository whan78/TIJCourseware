Here is the picture for the whole course. An object is a circle of services: state in the dark core, methods at the ports on the shell. Clients send messages — method calls — that aim only at the ports, never at the core. Watch the arrows, not my keyboard. And remember: the class is the cookie cutter; objects are the cookies. One seed I will grow at 5.6: objects no reference can reach are reclaimed by garbage collection. That shell has a name: encapsulation — and three more pillars follow.
