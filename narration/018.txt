The eight primitives. Bytes, shorts, ints, and longs for whole numbers; floats and doubles for decimals; char is a sixteen-bit unsigned number, so 'A' plus one is sixty-six — a recurring multiple-choice trap; boolean holds true or false only. Widening is automatic, narrowing needs a cast — full story at 3.5, one sentence today. Remember the coin model on the right: a primitive variable IS the value itself, while a reference is a claim ticket you exchange for the object. Primitives are values — but what about collections of things? Arrays.
