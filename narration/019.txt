Arrays are objects. new int bracket three creates an object on the heap; slots default to zero, false, or null; length is fixed at creation — here, four. The first three slots hold references to the Strings Ada, Bo, and Cy; the fourth slot points at nothing, and that is exactly where NullPointerException is born. Use the exam phrasing: the array itself is an object. Aggregate initialization rules come in chapter five; today, just the picture. Variables vanish at braces — objects do not. Scope versus lifetime is next.
