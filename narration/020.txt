Two different questions. Scope is where a name is visible — space. Lifetime is how long it exists — time. Braces end names, not objects: temp died at the brace, but the String it pointed to lived on. Objects die by garbage collection, not by braces — and only when nothing can reach them. Do not confuse name legality with object existence; that contrast is the exam short-answer phrasing, and it is exactly what RefBasics' nested block showed. Now the unit that holds it all: the class.
