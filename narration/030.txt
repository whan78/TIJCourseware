The ternary reads: condition, question mark, value if true, colon, value if false — and unlike if-else, it is an EXPRESSION: it produces a value you can store, here the verdict string. Perfect for verdict-style one-liners like pass or retry. One warning from me, and I mean it: do not nest ternaries.
