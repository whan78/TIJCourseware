Switch picks a case by value — here, the tens digit of the score. No break means execution FALLS THROUGH into the next case: silent, no error at all, and that is exactly what makes it dangerous — pitfall number two in this chapter. The one legal fall-through is the deliberate stacked labels: case ten, case nine share the A branch; everything else breaks. Goto exists in the language, but we never use it — and it is not examined; this is a favorite multiple-choice trap. Now build the whole thing with me.
