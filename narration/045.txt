Two votes — phrases one to three. First: c is a char, which f runs? Second: 10L is a long. Write your predictions, then we reveal. The verified answer: f(c) prints f(int) and f(10L) prints f(long). Widening beats everything: char widens to int, the smallest applicable type. If nothing widening fits, the compiler boxes; varargs is the last resort — that part is not on the exam ladder. The common wrong answer is f(double), because people reach for the biggest type. And note: overload resolution is a compile-time choice — we meet its run-time twin much later in the course. Follow-up, live: add f(char) and rerun — now it wins.
