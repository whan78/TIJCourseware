GC handles memory — only memory. Files, sockets, database connections: the garbage collector will never close them for you; you release them yourself. That is the attitude goal I care about most in this section: resource stewardship — every resource you open gets a release plan written before you open it. On the checklist: heap memory gets a check under GC's job; file handles, sockets and DB connections sit under your job with crosses and a red stamp: no automatic cleanup. We keep this framing and skip the finalize deep-dive — understand level, exam short answer. Now the exam-trap root of the whole course: initialization order.
