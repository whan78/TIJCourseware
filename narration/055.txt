Three forms. Brace lists like {1, 2, 3} are aggregate initialization — legal only at the declaration. new int[4] is dynamic: four slots, all defaulted to zero. Zoom on the re-assignment: c = new int[] { 5, 6 } — once the variable exists, bare braces are gone; re-assignment needs the new int[] form. And varargs: int... numbers means the caller passes any count, even zero — sum with no arguments prints 0 because the varargs becomes an empty array. The output is verified: 3 0 5, then true, then 10, then 0. Aggregate-plus-enum details stay in the notes. Quiz, then lab.
