A package is a named group of classes — the library unit. Fully-qualified names are long: net.mindview.util.Vector, and that length is the point — package names are unique across the world, which is why we build them from reversed domain names. import just shortens names: after the import, Vector alone is enough. Look at the two trees: net.mindview.util holding Vector, List and Sorter, and the sibling com.myproject holding Account. Classpath setup lives in the lab only. The real power arrives on the next slide: a package is not just organization — it is the access boundary. Inside and across packages, who may use what?
