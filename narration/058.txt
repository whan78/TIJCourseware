Four levels, four columns, one matrix to memorize. public works from anywhere. private only inside its own class. protected adds subclass access on top of package access. And the row I highlighted is the trap: no modifier at all is package-private — visible in the same package, invisible everywhere else. Say it plainly: no modifier does NOT mean public — big pitfall. Anchor back to the two trees from the previous slide: every column here is anchored to packages. This matrix is exam material, both multiple choice and code reading. Choosing the level is a design decision — that is the next slide.
