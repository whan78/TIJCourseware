One keyword, three kinds of no. final data: the value cannot change — and a blank final is assigned exactly once, inside the constructor. final method: it cannot be overridden — subclasses inherit it as-is. final class: it cannot be extended at all — the extends arrow is simply cut. The design sentence to carry with you: final classes trade extensibility for certainty — use sparingly. KP 7.9 is Understand, and the rationale behind final is Know level. Blank final stays a gloss today. Upcasting is the move that makes polymorphism possible — next.
