Class loading: the JVM brings a class in and initializes its statics once, before first use. Now the full ladder for a three-class chain — Animal, Dog, Puppy — drawn as three lanes through four stages. Stage one, each class loads on its own first use. Stage two, statics: Animal's, then Dog's, then Puppy's — once each, ever. Stage three, instance fields in order. Stage four, constructor bodies: Animal, then Dog, then Puppy. Base before derived — everywhere. Remember chapter five's three-rung ladder in the corner? Same ladder, more classes. And arrays are objects: they load and initialize through this same ladder too.
