This slide is the exam's center of gravity; if you photograph one slide today, make it this one. Binding is the act of connecting a call to the body that runs. Late binding means that choice happens at run time. At compile time we ask what MAY be called — that is the reference type, the blue arrows. At run time we ask which BODY executes — that is the object type, the orange arrow. The compiler checks the address exists; the runtime asks who actually answers the door. One caveat now, proven at 8.5: this applies to instance methods only — fields and statics do not polymorph.
