Live demo. The bad cast compiles fine — the compiler only sees a Shape, and a Triangle cast from Shape is plausible. Uncomment it, run it, and read the compiler's complaint together: ClassCastException — class Circle cannot be cast to class Triangle. When you meet this exception, ask the reflex question: who created the object? The instanceof guard is the safe pattern: guard, cast, use. And getClass().getSimpleName() tells the truth about what is really on the heap. Half-time: your project checkpoint.
