Remember KP 8.2: the abstract class exists so the late-bound call has something to aim at. An abstract class may hold state, concrete code, and promises; an abstract method is declared with no body, and every subclass must fill it. Why forbid instantiation? Because a promise without a body cannot run. Keep the model in mind: a form with some fields pre-filled and some fields blank. The illegal-instantiate moment is the next slide, right after the code.
