Welcome back. In Part One you learned to think in objects: classes, composition, inheritance, polymorphism, and interfaces. Part Two turns those ideas into working machinery. We start with inner classes, then spend most of our time on the standard library — containers, exceptions, strings, generics, I/O — and finish with concurrency and graphical interfaces. Everything still comes from Thinking in Java, fourth edition, chapters ten to twenty-two. Same rules as before: code first, read it aloud, predict the output.
