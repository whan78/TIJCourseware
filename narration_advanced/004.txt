Chapter ten, inner classes, four hours. The chapter is short but its idiom is everywhere. We move from plain inner classes, through dot-this and dot-new, to upcasting them behind interfaces, then anonymous classes, then static nested classes. The exam targets are two: explain the hidden outer-object reference, and read or write an anonymous inner class. By the end you should also be able to say precisely when a nested class should be static — that is a classic interview question.
