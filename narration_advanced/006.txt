Two pieces of syntax make the hidden link explicit. Inside an inner method, Outer.this is the outer object — notice back() returns it and the equality checks confirm each inner is welded to the outer that made it. Outside, the must-have is the new-in syntax: o1 dot new Inner. Read it as 'make an Inner belonging to o1'. Note the type written from outside is Outer.Inner, the fully qualified name. Predict the two booleans before I reveal them — then swap the objects and check you can still predict.
