Remember the factory pattern from chapter nine? We needed one named class per service plus one named factory class. Anonymous inner classes collapse both: the private constructor guarantees the only door is the factory, and the factory is just an expression. Count the anonymous classes — two, nested, and not one of them has a name. The consume method is unchanged from Part One, which is the point: clients program to interfaces while implementations shrink to where they are used. You will use exactly this shape for comparators in chapter seventeen.
