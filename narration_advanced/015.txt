Chapter eleven, five hours — together with generics it is the backbone of the term. The problem statement is on the divider: you cannot know at compile time how many objects you will need. Java answers with two interface trees: Collection, holding standalone objects, and Map, holding key-value pairs. We will touch every everyday implement­ation — ArrayList, LinkedList, HashSet, TreeSet, HashMap, PriorityQueue — and one traversal idea, Iterator. Exam-wise: pick the right container for a stated requirement, and trace iterator code exactly.
