Start with the pain that motivates everything. Before generics a container held Object, so anything fit — an Orange in your apple basket — and the failure surfaced as a ClassCastException wherever you happened to read the element, possibly weeks of code away from the bad add. Generics move the check to the compiler: the declared List-of-Apples rejects Oranges on the spot and hands back Apples with no cast. Note the diamond on the right-hand side — pure inference. Everything else this chapter stands on this shift: run-time surprise to compile-time guarantee.
