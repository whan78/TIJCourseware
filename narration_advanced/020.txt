Iteration is where polymorphism pays again. Look at the loop: nothing in it mentions ArrayList — iterator() returns something with hasNext and next, and the same loop works unchanged on a LinkedList or a TreeSet. The trap to drill: you must not call pets.remove() while iterating; use the iterator's own remove, which removes the element just returned and keeps the internal cursor consistent. ListIterator exists only for Lists and can move backwards — here we start at the end via listIterator(size). Trace the console output by hand before I show it.
