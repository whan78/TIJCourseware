LinkedList moonlights as every linear structure. Through push, pop, and peek it is a stack — last in, first out, so three comes off first. Through offer, poll, and peek via the Queue interface it is a queue — one entered first and leaves first, from the other end. Same object, two disciplines, purely by choice of method names. Also learn the two failure modes: the pair peek and poll return null on empty, while element and remove throw NoSuchElementException. Exam question: given this sequence of calls, what prints? Trace it now.
