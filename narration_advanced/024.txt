A queue is a discipline, not a data layout — hence two very different classes behind the same interface. LinkedList gives plain FIFO: one, two, three in arrival order. PriorityQueue instead serves by comparison — watch the loop print three, six, nine regardless of the order we offered them. By default the smallest comes out first; pass a reversed Comparator at construction for largest-first. Under the hood it is a binary heap — that is chapter seventeen territory, but you can already choose it correctly: whenever 'next most urgent' is the rule, this is the container.
