Summary of the container chapter. Two interface trees with generics typed at the declaration; three list/set/queue families each answering one question about ordering, duplicates, or discipline; and one traversal abstraction, the iterator, whose remove method is the only legal removal during a walk. The lab asks you to take the Shapes example and rank five containers against a workload description — it is a design-argument exercise, not a coding one.
