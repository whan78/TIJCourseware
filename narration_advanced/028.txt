Chapter twelve, four hours, and it is exam-heavy. The headline: exceptions turn error reporting into object passing. We will build the mental model — throw, catch, propagate — then the class hierarchy, then finally and cleanup, then the design question of which exceptions to declare. The two exam priorities are tracing try-catch-finally order exactly, and deciding whether a situation describes a checked exception or a RuntimeException.
