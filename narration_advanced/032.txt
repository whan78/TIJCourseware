Custom exceptions are deliberately boring: extend Exception and you inherit everything — message, stack trace, cause chaining. Most of the time the body is empty and you add constructors that forward the message to super. The real content is the DECISION of which parent to extend: Exception makes the failure checked, forcing callers to confront it; RuntimeException makes it optional, appropriate for programmer-error conditions. Design first, syntax second. In the lab you will add a severity field to this class and see handlers use it.
