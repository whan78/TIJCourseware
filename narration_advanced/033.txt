When an exception reaches you, it brings evidence. getMessage returns the constructor string; printStackTrace prints the whole call chain; and getStackTrace hands you the frames programmatically — here we print just method names and see f, g, main, newest first. Read traces bottom-up for the cause and top-down for where it surfaced. In real debugging this habit — actually reading the trace instead of panicking — is worth more than any other exception skill in this chapter.
