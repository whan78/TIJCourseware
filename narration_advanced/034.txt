finally answers one question: where do I put cleanup that must happen no matter what? The loop shows both paths — pass one throws, is caught, and finally still runs; pass two completes normally and finally still runs. Even a return inside try cannot cancel it. Typical tenants: close files, release locks, restore flags. Mention try-with-resources as the modern automation for AutoCloseable objects, but for the exam the rule is the raw one: finally always runs — and if finally itself throws, it can swallow the original exception, which is the next slide's warning.
