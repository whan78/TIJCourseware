Three professional moves and one famous bug. First, chaining: when you translate a low-level exception into your API's vocabulary, pass the original as the cause — the stack trace then shows both layers, and getCause recovers the detail. Second, plain rethrowing: log and throw the same object upward. Third — the trap — an exception raised inside finally silently REPLACES whatever try or catch threw. The word 'important' never reaches any handler; only 'trivial' survives. That is the exception-loss question that shows up on exams and in code reviews alike.
