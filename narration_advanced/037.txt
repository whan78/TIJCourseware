Summary in six lines, and note how much follows from the single fact that exceptions are objects. Mechanism: throw, unwind, catch. Ordering: child catches before parent. Design: checked for foreseeable external failures, unchecked for bugs. Cleanup in finally, with the replacement trap when finally itself throws. Custom types are cheap; the choice of parent is the design statement. And when translating between layers, keep the cause. The lab has you build a small parser that defines one checked exception and one runtime exception and defends the choice. Next chapter: the class everyone concatenates daily — Strings.
