Same output, wildly different cost. The plus form in a loop makes the compiler synthesize a fresh StringBuilder and a fresh String on every pass — quadratic behavior hidden behind innocent syntax. The explicit StringBuilder keeps ONE buffer and appends into it — linear. Know the rewrite rule for the exam, and know the sibling: StringBuffer is the synchronized ancestor, StringBuilder the faster default for local use. Simple rule to carry: concatenate two or three values with plus; assemble anything in a loop with StringBuilder.
