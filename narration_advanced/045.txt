Scanner finishes the string toolkit: it turns raw text into typed tokens with the hasNext-next pairing, from a file, a stream, or a plain string. Learn the pairing discipline — test before every read — and the token versus line distinction. The classic bug: after nextInt, the newline is still pending, so an immediate nextLine returns an empty string; consume it or use a consistent style. Under the hood Scanner is regex matching with conversion — which is why it sits in this chapter and not in I/O.
