Reflection, hands on the wheel. From one Class object you list constructors and public methods — note we filter out Object's boilerplate, since getMethods includes everything inherited. newInstance builds an object with no factory and no cast safety. getMethod resolves a specific overload by parameter types, and invoke performs the call — dynamic dispatch by hand. Checked exceptions thrown inside the called method come back wrapped, so handlers must unwrap the cause. This is exactly how testing frameworks call your test methods without knowing their names in advance.
