The payoff slide: dynamic proxies, where reflection becomes architecture. We define the interface and a real implementation, then a handler whose invoke method sees EVERY call before forwarding it. The proxy object itself is synthesized at run time by Proxy.newProxyInstance — nobody writes its class. Around the forwarded call you can log, enforce security, open transactions, or defer loading. When your lecturer later says 'Spring wraps your beans', this exact mechanism is what it means — worth two minutes of staring at the flow.
