Summary of chapter fourteen. The Class object is the protagonist: one per class, made by the loader, reachable three ways whose differences matter. instanceof and exact class equality answer different questions — usable-as versus exactly-is. Reflection is the same machinery opened to run-time discovery, with proxies as its architectural form. And the design guidance stands: polymorphism first, type tests sparingly, factories instead of cascades. The lab loads classes from a config file and lists their test methods — a tiny JUnit.
