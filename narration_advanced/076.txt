The whole toolkit on one screen, grouped by job: printing with the toString family; content comparison with deepEquals; filling and copying from the previous slide; sorting and searching from this one; plus asList, which you now recognize as the varargs-generics pattern. The bottom band is the judgment call: arrays remain the right tool for primitive bulk data and library interoperability, but for everything else — growth, searching, keyed access, polymorphism — collections win, which is exactly why the standard library itself is built on them.
