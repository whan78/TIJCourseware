The engine room. Take the key, call its hashCode — an int the object computes from its fields. The table folds that int into a bucket index, here with a mask over fifteen for a sixteen-slot table. The lookup story: hash to find the bucket, equals to find the element inside it. Two design facts follow. Uniform spread is everything — a function that dumps all keys into bucket seven reduces your O-one hash map to a linear list. And collisions are normal, not errors: the bucket holds both Rat and Cow and equals separates them. That division of labor is the contract slide next.
