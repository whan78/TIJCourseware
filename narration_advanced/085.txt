LinkedHashMap threads a linked list through the hash table, so iteration follows insertion while lookups stay hashed. The second half is the showpiece: construct it in access-order mode and override removeEldestEntry, and you have a working least-recently-used cache — get refreshes recency, the map evicts its oldest access victim, and the final printout shows y gone and x surviving because we touched it. Interviews love this question; now you know it is a three-line override on a standard class, not a data-structures exercise.
