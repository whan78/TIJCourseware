The Collections class is to collections what Arrays was last chapter: a bag of static tools. Three groups. Wrappers — unmodifiable and synchronized views; the key exam fact is that the view rejects modification while the backing collection keeps working: a lens, not a copy. Factories — empty and singleton collections, cheaper than allocating one-element containers. And order operations — sort, reverse, shuffle, min, max. Design-wise, unmodifiableList is how an API hands out internal state without surrendering it; pair it with defensive copying for the paranoid cases.
