A quieter corner with a loud consequence: reference strength. Ordinary fields and collections hold strong references — the garbage collector cannot touch the object, which is exactly why a plain HashMap used for metadata about foreign objects is a leak: the map keeps every key alive forever. WeakHashMap holds its keys weakly: when the last strong reference elsewhere disappears, the entry evaporates on the next collection. That makes it the correct tool for caches and annotation maps keyed by objects you do not own. Exam-wise, know the four strengths and the leak scenario.
