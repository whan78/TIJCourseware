Chapter eighteen, four hours. The I/O library intimidates because it has dozens of classes — until you see the two orthogonal ideas: byte streams versus character streams, and decoration, where each wrapper adds one capability. After the mental model we do files, buffered text, data streams, object serialization, and a brief look at the new-I/O buffer machinery. Exam targets: identify what a chain of stream decorators does, and the serialization rules for static and transient fields.
