The design idea that organizes dozens of classes: decoration. Every stream wrapper holds another stream, forwards the boring calls inward, and adds exactly one capability. File input sources bytes; the buffered layer batches them for speed; the data layer exposes readInt and friends. Read chains inside-out — innermost is the source, outermost is the richest interface. You have met this before without the name: chapter ten's callbacks and the reader-writer pairs in every tutorial are the same wrapper pattern. Once you see it, the class forest stops being memorization.
