When data is numeric and voluminous, text is the wrong format: binary streams write exact primitives in fixed widths. The write side and read side mirror each other — and the order IS the protocol: readDouble after writeInt reads garbage. writeUTF deserves a comment: it writes a length-prefixed modified-UTF-8 string, so it pairs only with readUTF, not with other tools' encodings. This format family is what Object serialization on the next slide builds on — and where its fragility starts.
