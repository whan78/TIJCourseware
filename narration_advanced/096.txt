Serialization turns a live object graph into bytes and back — the machinery behind RMI, sessions, and every 'save game' feature. The rules are the exam: Serializable is a marker with no methods; transient fields are skipped and come back as defaults — our secret prints null; static fields never serialize because they live with the class, not the object; and the write is deep — every reachable non-transient field must itself be Serializable or a NotSerializableException flies. Mention versioning briefly: changing the class after saving bytes is where serialVersionUID enters — lab material.
