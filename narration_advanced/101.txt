Chapter nineteen, three hours, and it upgrades an old acquaintance. The chapter-five enum was syntax convenience; the full enum is a class with a fixed instance set — you cannot add one at run time, which is the whole safety point. We cover the standard self-generated methods, constructors and methods of your own, switch integration, the specialized EnumSet and EnumMap collections, and the showpiece: constant-specific method implementations — polymorphism inside a single enum type.
