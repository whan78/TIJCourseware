The fine print. Equality by double-equals is not a bug here — each constant exists exactly once, so identity comparison is correct and idiomatic. Inheritance: enum quietly extends java.lang.Enum, and single inheritance means an enum cannot also extend your class — but implementing interfaces is fine, which enables the constant-specific behavior coming on the next slide. Static import shortens case labels in larger switch statements. And a warning to pre-empt a classic mistake: ordinal is positional, so reordering constants reorders meaning — persist names, not ordinals.
