Two collections exist just for enums, and both cheat brilliantly. EnumSet packs membership into a long — a bit per constant — so contains, of, range, and complement are single machine words' worth of work; note range is inclusive on both ends. EnumMap is an array indexed by ordinal: no hashing at all, and iteration comes out in declaration order, as the printout shows. The constructor needs the class literal — that is chapter fourteen's Class object giving the erasure-free runtime identity. When your keys are enum constants, these beat HashSet and HashMap on every axis.
