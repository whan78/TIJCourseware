The showpiece. Declare getInfo abstract inside the enum, then give each constant its own body — under the hood each becomes an anonymous inner class, chapter ten paying another dividend. A loop over values dispatches polymorphically: every constant answers with its own implementation and there is not one switch statement in sight. Compare with the classic enum-plus-switch version: adding a constant here forces you to write its behavior inline — the compiler enforces completeness — while the switch version compiles happily with a missing case. That completeness guarantee is the design argument.
