Defining an annotation is one keyword with a hat: @interface. Elements look like methods without bodies and may carry defaults, which makes them optional at use sites. The real design content is the pair of meta-annotations on top. Target restricts where the annotation may legally appear — methods here, and the compiler enforces it. Retention decides the lifetime, and the three levels matter: source dies at compilation, class is the default that never reaches the JVM, and runtime is what reflection can see — our next slide requires runtime. Value as a special element name enables the shorthand form.
