Chapter twenty-one, five hours, the conceptual summit of the course. Two threads sharing one field can corrupt each other in ways that never reproduce on the instructor's machine — so the discipline matters more than the syntax. We build up: creating threads, the Executor service, lifecycle controls, then the shared-state problem with synchronized and Lock, volatile's narrower promise, wait/notify cooperation, deadlock, and the state diagram that ties it together. Exam priorities: trace a race, and decide which tool fits a stated sharing pattern.
