In production you do not manage Threads; you submit tasks to an ExecutorService. newCachedThreadPool reuses idle threads and creates as needed — fine for short tasks; fixed pools cap concurrency for heavy work; the single-threaded executor is secretly a synchronization tool, guaranteeing one-at-a-time without locks. Two contract points: execute accepts a Runnable, and shutdown stops task acceptance while letting running tasks finish — it is a close, not a kill. Note the console: five liftoffs interleave arbitrarily, which is the point of the demo and the first taste of non-determinism.
