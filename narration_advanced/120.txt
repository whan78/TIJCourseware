The adjustment knobs, with honest warnings. Yield suggests letting another thread run; priorities rank preference — both are hints the operating system may ignore, so algorithms built on them are broken by construction. Sleep pauses without releasing any lock the thread holds, and InterruptedException is the cooperative cancellation signal — catch it and clean up. Daemons earn the exam question: mark a thread daemon before start, and the JVM will exit when the last non-daemon thread ends, killing daemons mid-statement — which is why their finally blocks are not guaranteed.
