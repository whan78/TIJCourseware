Two coordination essentials. Join makes the caller wait for a target thread's completion, with an optional timeout — the rendezvous primitive before we reach the richer wait and notify machinery. Exceptions behave surprisingly: an uncaught exception in a worker thread terminates only that thread; main continues in blissful ignorance, which has lost many production logs. The uncaught-exception handler is the net — install it for any thread whose death matters. Exam format: given sleep and join timings, predict the exact interleaving of the three output lines.
