The guards, in escalating power. synchronized on a method locks the receiver; a synchronized block locks whatever object you name — which is the granularity lever: two methods on different locks run in parallel, as the console contrast shows, sometimes that is the design, sometimes the bug. ReentrantLock is the explicit cousin — lock and unlock bracketing a try-finally, with advanced options like tryLock that gives up instead of waiting. And for the single-counter problem that opened the chapter, the atomic classes are the clean answer — incrementAndGet is lock-free and correct. Rule of thumb: simplest tool that covers the whole critical section.
