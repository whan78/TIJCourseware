Mutual exclusion is half of coordination; the other half is handing work between threads. wait is called while holding the object's lock and does two things at once: suspends the thread and RELEASES the lock — otherwise the waker could never enter. notifyAll wakes every waiter, who then re-compete for the lock and re-check the condition — which is why the check is a while and never an if: by the time a woken thread runs, the condition may be false again. Our wax-and-buffer pair produces strict alternation. For queues, prefer the library's BlockingQueue — wait/notify is the machinery under it.
