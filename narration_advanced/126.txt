Deadlock: thread A holds the first lock and waits for the second; B holds the second and waits for the first. Nobody moves; no exception; your server just stops. The four Coffman conditions on the slide are the diagnosis AND the treatment menu — deadlock requires all four, so break any one. In practice the weapon is the last line: global lock ordering — everywhere in the codebase, acquire locks in the same sequence, and the circular wait cannot form. Exam format: given two synchronized methods calling each other, spot the cycle and propose the fix.
