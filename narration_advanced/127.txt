The lifecycle map that ties the chapter together. New until start; runnable when the scheduler may choose it — running and ready are lumped together in Java's model; blocked when a lock is unavailable — the synchronized world; waiting when in wait, join, or lock-waiting, and timed waiting when a timeout was given; terminated when run returns. Thread dumps and every profiler speak these six words, so learn to name the state of any thread from its last operation: a thread inside a synchronized block it cannot enter is blocked, inside wait is waiting. That vocabulary is the debugging toolkit for the lab.
