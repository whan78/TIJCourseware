Swing's mental model is a tree. At the root a JFrame — the operating-system window with its title bar and close button. Inside sits the content pane, the container whose layout manager places everything you add. Then the components: buttons and text fields are leaves; JPanel is an intermediate container that itself holds a layout, which is how complex screens compose from simple regions; JScrollPane wraps anything that may overflow. Events and painting flow up this tree, which is why a button inside two panels still reaches your handler. The next slide decides WHERE children land — that is the layout manager's job.
