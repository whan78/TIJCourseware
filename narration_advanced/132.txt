Placement without pixel math: layout managers are strategy objects — chapter nine again — each with a placement policy. FlowLayout runs a row and wraps like text; BorderLayout divides the window into five regions where center absorbs all extra space, which is exactly why text areas behave; GridLayout cuts equal cells. The professional pattern is composition: nest panels, each with its own manager, and complex screens emerge from three simple rules. Null layouts with absolute coordinates feel tempting on day one and are broken on day two — different screen sizes and fonts shatter them.
