The event model in one working program. A button is an event source that keeps a list of listeners; a click constructs an ActionEvent and calls actionPerformed on each. Look at the registration line and recognize it: the lambda is exactly the anonymous inner class from chapter ten — the framework supplies the interface, you supply the behavior, no new concepts required. Two operational notes: setVisible starts the event dispatch loop, and all GUI mutations should happen on that single thread — hence invokeLater for construction. Everything else in Swing is composition of what you have already seen.
